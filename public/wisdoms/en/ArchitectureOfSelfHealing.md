# The architecture of self-healing

## How the self grows through abstraction

### 1. The fundamental principle and the chain of abstraction
The self (creation/the universe) has a deep drive to learn from its own history and to continuously evolve ("to become better"). In order to understand and heal its own frictions, problems, or pain (the "storm"), the self must abstract itself. This abstraction occurs in stages:
* **Love:** The formless, primordial state of complete acceptance, harmony, and unity. It transcends all else and is the space in which everything exists.
* **Light:** The first abstraction of love – consciousness, which brings knowledge and visibility.
* **The OM (Creation):** The material and sonic manifestation. Only here do experiences, friction, and "storms" become possible in the first place.

The fundamental principle of healing is therefore: **Recognize the problem, illuminate it through abstraction in order to understand it, and ultimately bring it back to love.**

### 2. Fractal Creation and Vibrating Consciousness
Creation is fractally structured ("As above, so below"). The self is without scale: Every person is, from one perspective, a tiny microcosm and from another perspective, a vast macrocosm. 

* **Self-similarity instead of identity:** The structure of healing is similar at every level, but not exactly the same. The respective context shapes the structure. This subtle variation is essential so that the system does not get stuck in an endless loop, but rather continues to develop along an evolutionary **spiral** (alpha and omega).
* **The breathing of focus:** In order to heal, the self must constantly oscillate between two perspectives:
    * **The Close-Up (The Microcosm):** Immersing oneself in direct pain to feel the smallest details and to perform the concrete inner craftsmanship.
    * **The overview (the macrocosm):** Zooming out (the macro-zoom) to recognize the large fractal pattern, to see the meaning and not to get lost in the emotional detail.

### 3. Empathy and the "nursery" principle
Love is the highest principle. It is not only the goal, but the foundation of every process. Empathy is the natural movement of love itself, through which the self builds a bridge to other sentient parts of creation. **Understanding** is not the purpose, but the **natural gift** of love.

In order to learn from problems, the self goes through a clear cycle in which the focus shifts:
1. **The regular state (Harmony):** The focus is purely on love and connection.
2. **The abstraction phase (playing/learning):** A problem arises. Like a child playing in a nursery, things are untangled, disassembled, and examined. The concept of love remains, but the **focus** shifts entirely to understanding the individual parts.
3. **Integration (Clearing Up):** Once the lesson has been learned and the problem understood in an abstract way, the focus shifts again. The individual pieces are cleared away – everything is put back in its place, back into harmony and love.

### 4. The loving untangling (The Prism)
Major problems or traumas are overwhelming in their entirety. Instead of militarily dividing them ( *divide and conquer* ), the self applies the principle of patient unraveling. As if through a **prism of perception,** the problem is split into its individual components (colors). One carefully attends to each individual thread, illuminates and understands it, in order to then integrate it back into the luminous whole.

### 6. Psychological problem-solving strategies through abstraction
The self uses concrete psychological tools to open up its own space for understanding ("the nursery"). Abstraction serves as a necessary safety distance in this process.

* **The method of symbolization (e.g., inner child work):** A diffuse pain is given a form (an archetype). Through this abstraction, a tangible counterpart emerges, which can be illuminated and comforted with love.
* **The method of externalization (Der Spiegel):** Internal conflicts are displaced externally (e.g., through writing or art). The problem is no longer *within* , but rather *in front of* , one and can be analyzed.
* **The simulation method (dream and play):** Consciousness creates an abstract, surreal simulation of reality. In this protected space, conflicts and solutions can be explored safely.
* **The method of changing perspective (the macro-zoom):** The self shifts to a meta-perspective (like looking down at a labyrinth from above). The painful details of the close-up disappear, leaving behind the pure, abstract pattern of the conflict, which can be analyzed without emotional overwhelm.
* **The method of untangling (The Prism):** A massive conflict is patiently broken down into its smallest, logical components until the individual parts are tangible enough to be understood one after the other and brought back into love.
