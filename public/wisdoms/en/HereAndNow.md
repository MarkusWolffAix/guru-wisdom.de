# The here and now
## The transformative power of the present

*Elements, time and the here and now*

## 1. The Four Elements as a Guidepost

* **Fire (Sun, God):** Fire represents orientation. It is the divine light that illuminates our vision, shows us the way, and ultimately navigates us to our true destination.
* **Air (wind, breath):** It is the driving force, the prana. Just as the wind fills the sail of a ship, breath gives us the necessary life energy to even begin the journey to our destination.
* **Water (Time):** Water symbolizes the constant flow of time. When it flows as a stream over many stones, it purifies itself. Similarly, time shapes us: Even a rocky, difficult past, like water, has cleansed us, matured us, and made our souls more beautiful.
* **The Earth (The Support and the Vessel):** The Earth is the solid foundation, the riverbed, and the bowl. It is what securely holds and shapes the flowing water of time and gives us stability.

## 2. Determining our location

The interplay of these cosmic forces gives rise to the law governing our life's journey: **direction + time = location.** The direction we navigate in (the sun), combined with the time we spend traveling this path (the flowing water), precisely determines our current physical and spiritual location.

## 3. The Great Cycle of Time

Time is not merely a consumption, but a harmonious cycle between us and the Earth:

* **The gift:** When we enter life, the earth provides us with the space and the "riverbed". In a sense, it lends us time so that our water can flow at all.
* **The maturation process:** We use this time to flow over our stones, gather experiences, and grow from them.
* **The return:** We don't keep this time to ourselves. With every passing moment and every conscious exhale, we return the time we have lived to the Earth. She, who initially gave us time, gently receives these mature, experienced moments back into herself and preserves them for eternity. It is a constant, peaceful giving and receiving.

## 4. Meditation: Completion in the "here and now"

In conscious meditation, all these insights merge into the simple yet powerful act of a single breath:

* **The "Here" (Inhaling):** We breathe in the air deeply and absorb the prana – pure solar energy. This energy, which has guided us on our path, firmly anchors us in our current spatial location. We arrive. **We are Here.**
* **The "Now" (exhaling):** We exhale and surrender to the steady, purifying flow of time. In exhaling, we let go. We cling neither to water that has passed nor to water that will future. We sink into the safe vessel of the earth and, in this very second, give it back a piece of our lived time. **We are in the Now.**

Through this constant rhythm, we rest perfectly grounded in the eye of the storm – carried by the earth, cleansed by the water, filled by the air, and guided by the fire.
