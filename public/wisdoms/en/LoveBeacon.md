# Birth from the Divine
## The Lighthouse of Love

We do not enter this world as blank slates, but from the pure state of oneness – perfect yoga. Birth is not a separation from the Divine, but a wondrous act of manifestation. Our consciousness initially exists as an abstract form in the spiritual realm before finding its way into a physical body as a new birth or rebirth. On this journey through the subtle dimensions down into the material world, there is a driving, guiding force: love.

Universal love acts like a cosmic compass. It guides consciousness so that it finds precisely the body and environment that correspond to its inner vibration. It is fascinating to observe that this energetic connection often leads us back to the same geographical region. The soul remembers the Earth's resonance, the climate, and the soil that has been home and a place of growth for many lifetimes.

In this mystical process, expectant parents play a very special role: They are the compassionate lighthouse. A lighthouse doesn't force anything or anyone onto its course; it stands firmly rooted in the earth and tirelessly shines its light into the distance. "Sympathetic" in its deepest sense means an energetic resonance. Parental love emits a clear, specific frequency. It calls to divine consciousness and offers it a safe haven. Drawn by this warming resonance, consciousness navigates safely through the ocean of infinity directly into the loving arms of physical existence.

It is a comforting and empowering thought: We are here because we followed the light. Love called us, and love received us.
