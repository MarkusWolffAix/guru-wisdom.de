# The secret of why
## From childlike wonder to the glue of connection

Between the ages of three and five, a tireless urge awakens in human consciousness: the world is no longer simply accepted, but profoundly questioned. A child's "Why?" is far more than simply gathering information. It is an attempt to grasp the hidden threads of cause and effect and to weave their own internal model of reality.

But anyone who listens closely will soon realize that this questioning unfolds in layers. Behind the initial "Why?" usually lies pure, unbiased interest in the subject. It's the pure beginner's mind, eager to understand the mechanics of the world. 

However, if a second "Why?" follows shortly after, the nature of the question changes. It is no longer primarily about the factual topic, but rather an emotional invitation. The child is seeking affection. They intuitively recognize that their questions capture the undivided attention of a loved one. If this emotional offering is overlooked and the child is instead met with dry facts, a third or fourth "Why?" inevitably leads to frustration. The question becomes an expression of an unmet need for presence and love.

The true answer to such a "Why?" therefore doesn't lie in a dictionary explanation. It lies in the time spent together. Knowledge and understanding demand to be experienced. Whether gazing through a telescope together in a planetarium, attempting a small physics experiment, or digging your hands deep into the moist soil of a vegetable garden – abstract facts transform into vivid memories. Such shared, wonderful experiences act as a powerful glue. They not only bind knowledge deeply into our consciousness but also strengthen our relationship with one another. 

Knowledge doesn't mechanically "stick" to us; it's emotionally anchored through the joy of discovery and a palpable sense of security. The shared presence elevates mere learning to an act of love.

If this form of resonance can be preserved and cultivated into adulthood, it opens the space for the purest and most intense form of interpersonal connection. When two people are gripped by the same beautiful feelings at the exact same moment, they resonate like two perfectly tuned instruments. In such precious moments of perfect synchronicity, when one feels they have found a true soulmate, no further explanation is needed. The shared feeling itself is the absolute answer, the unshakeable glue that binds two souls together. The boundaries between I and you blur, and the tireless questioning of "Why?" finds its perfect peace in the quiet, profound security of shared experience.
