# The Winning Day
## A Morning Manifesto

 The core themes of the text form a clear blueprint for a successful morning: deep gratitude immediately after waking up, the conscious setting of goals and intentions, the building of physical and mental strength through exercise and healthy eating, and strict protection from negative influences such as news and social media in the early morning. 

The subconscious mind is effectively programmed for focus, gratitude, and a drive to act, directly from sleep. Over the past few years, I've been woken up irregularly and seemingly at random by this song on Spotify.
