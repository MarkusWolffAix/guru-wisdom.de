# Ascension Day
## The Universal Path of Transformation

The Ascension of Christ marks far more than just a historical date in the religious calendar. It is a universal symbol for the soul's journey and the transition from the finite to the infinite. After overcoming duality, consciousness spends exactly 40 days in the intermediate realm to finally detach itself from material weight.

### The 40-Day Space: Maturation of the Spirit

The number 40 is a sacred code in the world's wisdom traditions. It is the period of time required to fully transcend an old identity and integrate a new way of being. These 40 days are not merely waiting, but an active phase of integration in which experience becomes inner certainty.

### The Return to the Source

Ascension is not a journey to a distant place, but rather the awakening to omnipresence. By releasing its connection to the physical form, consciousness returns to its original, undivided state. The drop realizes that it has always been the entire ocean. This return to the source is the ultimate act of liberation and completion.
