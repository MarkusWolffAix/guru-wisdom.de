# Historical timeline
## Occupiers & Influences of Alexandria

* **331 BC – Founding:** Founded by Alexander the Great and designed by his architect Deinocrates.
* **305–30 BC – Ptolemaic Dynasty:** Alexandria becomes the capital and flourishes as a center of Hellenism. It is the golden age of culture and science (Library of Alexandria, Lighthouse of Pharos).
* **30 BC – 395 AD – Roman Empire:** The city becomes an important Roman province. A time of the "Pax Romana", characterized by a strongly multicultural society.
* **395–641 AD – Byzantine Empire:** Alexandria becomes an important center of early Christianity, but also experiences intense religious conflicts.
* **641–1250 AD – Islamic Expansion & Caliphate:** After the Arab conquest, the capital was moved to Fustat (near present-day Cairo). This led to a loss of political importance and the gradual Arabization of the city.
* **1250–1517 AD – Mamluk Sultanate:** A rather turbulent period characterized by wars, economic decline, but also by the fortification and defense of the city.
* **1517–1801 AD – Ottoman Empire:** Alexandria remains an important strategic port, but experiences a long period of stagnation overall.
* **1798–1801 AD – French & British Intervention:** Struggle for Egypt between European powers (Napoleon's campaign). This period also marks the beginning of modern Egyptology (discovery of the Rosetta Stone).
* **1805–Today – Modernity & Independence:** Beginning with Muhammad Ali Pasha, the city underwent a massive reconstruction. It developed into the modern, cosmopolitan metropolis it is today in the Egyptian Republic.
