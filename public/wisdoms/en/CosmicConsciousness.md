# Birth from Love
## Consciousness and the Will of the Sentient Being

From the deepest essence of love, God was born—a process that continues to this day. It manifests an all-encompassing consciousness in the universe, which in its purest form is God.

From a broader perspective, it often becomes apparent that higher, divine entities work through boundless empathy. They work through "your God" and thus directly touch you, the sentient being.

The regular state of this being can be described as a circle around the sun—the manifested God, who can seemingly act and govern as he pleases. At least, this is the underlying concept.

But how exactly can this universal influence be experienced in one's own life? God, or this all-pervading consciousness, constantly influences your thoughts, your words, and your actions. You are continually offered thoughts—bright as well as dark, constructive as well as destructive. You always have the choice of which of these impulses you accept. From the consequences of this inner choice, deep love can grow, or conflict can arise.

Often we consider our thoughts, our words, and our actions to be our own, completely independent creations. This is true in a certain sense, and yet simultaneously an illusion. The crucial reflection that leads us to the truth is therefore:

How often do you *truly* think, speak, and act consciously, and therefore more authentically?
