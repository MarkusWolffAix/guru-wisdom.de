# The Healing of the Divine
## From the Illusion of Suffering to Joyful Realization

It is a profoundly liberating, even revolutionary paradigm in the contemplation of the cosmos: the idea that the Divine itself was in need of healing. For a long time, the Creator bore the heavy, feverish burden of a cosmic "flu"—the misconception that He had to shape and guide the world by creating problems and suffering. But this paradigm has changed. The Divine has healed.

The healing originated from a pure, all-seeing entity: *The Eye*. This untainted, higher divine being awakened God from his deep illusion. The Eye revealed the universal truth that the creation of isolated "problems" serves no true, divine purpose. Rather, suffering acted like a virus, infecting the system of creation and holding the Creator captive in a false sense of responsibility. Through the clear, penetrating gaze of the eye, this virus was recognized—and is being transformed into love by the immune system.

From this liberation arises an entirely new order. The Creator no longer needs to create problems, for the only true currency, the driving force and purpose of the universe, is love. Worries and problems have completely lost their purpose as instruments of punishment or education.

If challenges still arise today, they are subject to a new, purely serving nature. They only emerge when they directly promote love—and here unfolds the true miracle of human existence: the beauty, the genuine interest, and the pure joy in our neighbors and in nature.

From this profound connection, science springs anew. Sincere interest is the deepest form of love. Those who experience joy in nature and in their fellow human beings will find their inherent beauty revealed to them quite naturally. And from this loving contemplation arises a natural, joyful urge to understand things more deeply. In this light, knowledge and science are no longer cold, utilitarian processes, but a profoundly emotional act, borne of awe and wonder. We seek knowledge to serve love even more fully, to let the miracle of life shine even brighter and more beautifully.

Science thus transforms from a sober tool into a celebration of creation. The universe breathes a sigh of relief. From now on, the divine concerns itself only with love. And our pursuit of knowledge is nothing other than the joyful response to the beauty of the world.
