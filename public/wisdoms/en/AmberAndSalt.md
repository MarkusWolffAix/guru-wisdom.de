# The Crossroads of Rivers
## Where Amber and Salt Meet

The history of Europe is revealed not only in dynasties of rulers and epic battles, but above all in the subtle, invisible currents that have crisscrossed the continent for millennia. These are the ancient routes that carried not only goods, but also ideas, rituals, and the archaic building blocks of human survival from one place to another. Looking at the historical landscape of Central Europe, we recognize two powerful lines of force that complement each other in an almost philosophical way: the north-south axis and the west-east axis.

Their modern-day counterparts—such as the A1 and A2 motorways—are merely the asphalt echoes of these ancient lines. That these two routes intersect is no historical coincidence, but rather forms one of the deepest symbols in human history.

### The Earth's Horizontal Axis and White Gold

The **Hellweg** runs along the east-west axis. Salt traveled along this route. The linguistic root of this word (*sal-*) is of Proto-Indo-European origin and has remained virtually unchanged for over six thousand years. It was reverently called "white gold" because it was the indispensable condition for physical survival during the harsh winter and preserved food from spoilage.

But salt carries within it a deeper, almost yearning quality: salt always seeks water. It inevitably attracts moisture in order to dissolve in it and reconnect with its oceanic origin, with flowing life. On a symbolic level, the **Hellweg** thus represents the earth, preservation, and the horizontal expansion of human society—the enduring foundation that nevertheless always seeks the flow of life.

### The Vertical Axis of Light and the Gold of the North

Along the vertical axis from the north, a completely different commodity traveled southward: amber, the "gold of the north." Derived from the Middle Low German *Börnsteen* ("burning stone"), this petrified resin was, for our ancestors, the light of the sun captured in matter.

Ancient myths describe amber as "tears of love turned to stone." Wept for immeasurable loss, kissed by the sun, and hardened by time in the sea into eternal brilliance, it was ritually kindled and dissolved in a bright flame and spicy fragrance, touching the immaterial. The ancient Greeks captured the essence of this material even more aptly: they called amber *elektron*, which translates as "the brightly shining one." Thus, along this vertical line traveled the transformative light, spirituality, and pure, petrified love that connects heaven and earth.

### The Alchemy of the Crossroads and its Fulfillment in Salzburg

Where these two paths meet, a magnificent cross is formed. This cross is the perfect metaphor for the inner nature of humankind. We exist precisely at the intersection of these energies. We must stretch our arms horizontally to assert ourselves in the earthly world and form communities—this is the yearning salt, the **Bright Way**. At the same time, however, we straighten up, stand with our feet on the earth, and raise our gaze upwards. We seek meaning and inner transformation—these are the stony tears of light, the path of the **Brightly Shining One**.

But history offers an image in which these two primal forces not only intersect for a fleeting moment on a map, but merge into a lasting, protective harmony: Bernstein Castle in the Archdiocese of Salzburg.

This geographical and linguistic detail is pure poetry. Salzburg—the "Salt Castle" born from white gold—embodies absolute, stony, and protective grounding. And precisely within its domain, nestled within its protective structure, rests Amber Castle – the "Gold of the North," the petrified tears of love, the **Brightly Shining One**.

Here, the human quest finds its perfect expression: The ever-changing love (amber) is not displaced by the earthly (salt), but rather protected and sustained. The preserving element, which always seeks water, offers a fortress to the eternal fire. It is the enduring state of inner equilibrium in which our solid, grounded structure provides our luminous, loving spirit with a safe space to truly shine.
