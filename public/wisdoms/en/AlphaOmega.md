# Alpha and Omega
## The eternal cycle of beginning and end

This document summarizes the historical, linguistic, and biological connection between the first letters of our alphabet, Germanic myths, and the wild aurochs.

---

## 1. Alpha and Omega: From Ox to Eternity

The two letters began as practical tools and have transformed into one of the most powerful symbols in human history.

* **Its Phoenician origin:** The Greek alphabet comes from the Phoenicians. Their symbol *"Aleph"* meant "ox" and was depicted as an ox's head. The Greeks adopted this symbol and made it their first vowel: **Alpha** .
* **The invention of Omega:** For a long "O" sound, the Greeks later invented a completely new letter, **Omega** (literally: "large O"). Since it was the last addition, it was placed at the very end of the alphabet.
* **The symbol of totality:** In the biblical *book of Revelation* , the author used the metaphor "I am the Alpha and the Omega, the First and the Last." The letters thus merged into a universal symbol for infinity and the totality of being.
* **Today:** In German, this has evolved into the idiom **"Das A und O"** , which describes the essential core of a matter.

---

## 2. Etymology: The aurochs and the “double bully”

The story of the aurochs is also the story of a forgotten word.

* **The aurochs:** In old Germanic languages (such as Old High German or Old Norse), *aur* was simply the name for the enormous, wild European ox.
* **The origin of the word aurochs:** As wild cattle became increasingly rare due to hunting, people no longer knew what to do with the simple word *"Ur"* (aurochs). To clarify, they added the word "Ochse" (ox).
* **Tautology:** Through sound shifts, the "Ur-Ochsen" (primitive ox) became the **aurochs** . From a linguistic-historical perspective, this is a tautology, because literally translated it simply means "ox-ox".

---

## 3. Germanic Mythology: The Ox in the Runes

The Germanic peoples used their runic alphabet, the **Futhark** , not only for writing but also for rituals. The cow played a central role in the very beginning of the alphabet:

* **Fehu (The 1st Rune):** Represented the domesticated ox. It symbolized civilized prosperity, material possessions, and wealth.
* **Uruz (The 2nd Rune):** Represented the wild aurochs (the Ur). It formed the counterpoint and symbolized archaic life force, physical strength, and the courage for transformation (often linked to the initiation rite of hunting).

---

## 4. The Tauros Program: The Return of the Ur

The last true aurochs died in Poland in 1627. But its legacy lives on.

* **The genetic basis:** Since humans domesticated the aurochs, its genetic material flows in all our domestic cattle today.
* **The project:** As part of the modern, scientific *Tauros program* (since 2008), primitive cattle breeds from Southern Europe are being selectively crossbred and compared with ancient aurochs DNA.
* **Ecological goal:** This is about nature conservation ( *rewilding* ). These selectively bred specimens are intended to act as ecological "landscape architects," keeping European wild areas open and promoting the biodiversity of plants and insects.
