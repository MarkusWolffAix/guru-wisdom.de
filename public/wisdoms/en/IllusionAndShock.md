# The Healing Shock
## The Awakening of Consciousness After the Bang

There's a bang. On a grand scale—like the cosmic Big Bang—as well as in the small details of our daily lives. Often, we move through the world carelessly and almost recklessly, much like a driver who irresponsibly and numbly presses the accelerator. But what exactly triggers the sudden, big bang? It's the hard, unavoidable collision of our inner illusions with unvarnished reality. Consciousness often clings to constructs, expectations, and habits that can no longer withstand the actual truth. When this artificial shell is shattered by an external event, the pent-up tension between illusion and reality is released in a massive shock.

In precisely this fraction of a second, the form loses its cohesion. Just as a vehicle disintegrates into its individual parts upon impact, consciousness, too, is violently torn from its moorings. This profound sense of shock is the ultimate experience of isolation: Consciousness abruptly loses its connection to the greater whole, to the protective order. One falls into a seemingly weightless, yet deeply frightening space of absolute separation.

But when the sound of the impact fades, this moment after the bang reveals its true potential. It is like an old pocket watch falling to the ground and shattering into pieces. The rigid casing breaks open, and suddenly the delicate, complex interior—the hidden truth that ticked unseen in the background—becomes fully visible. The bang was thus not merely an act of destruction, but a radical revelation. The shell had to shatter so that the unprotected essence of our being could be exposed.

Amidst the wreckage of this moment of revelation, our true creative power unfolds. We are faced with a choice: Do we surrender to the chaos and discard the seemingly worthless fragments? Or do we regulate ourselves, accept the shock, and view the revealed truth with new eyes? If we choose to repair what is broken with deep devotion and love, far more than mere repair occurs. Through this conscious act of healing, we re-tie the broken bond. We reconnect with love, with God, and with universal oneness. From the painful crash awakens a consciousness no longer trapped in illusions, but truly rooted in the whole.
