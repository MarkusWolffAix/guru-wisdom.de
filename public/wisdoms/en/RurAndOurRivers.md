# Rur and Our
## The path of water from the High Fens to the North Sea

A geographical and creative journey through the river systems of the tri-border region:

**1. The Rur-Meuse system (westward direction)**
* **Rur:** Originates in the High Fens in Belgium and flows into the Netherlands, where it empties into the **Meuse** near Roermond.
* **Wurm:** Originates in the Aachen city forest from several springs and flows north, where it empties into the Rur.
* **Urft:** It originates in the Zitterwald forest in the northern Eifel region and also flows into the Rur river (after passing the Urft Dam).
* **Göhl:** A tributary that rises near Aachen-Lichtenbusch and flows directly into the Meuse near Maastricht.

**2. The Our-Rhine system (east/south direction)**
* **Our:** It also originates in the High Fens. We've established that it was often spelled **"Ur"** in Germany in the past, but is now officially called "Our". It forms a long border river and flows into the Sauer near Wallendorf.
* **Sauer:** It originates in the Belgian Ardennes and flows on to Wasserbillig, where it empties into the **Moselle** .
* The **Moselle River** originates at the Col de Bussang mountain pass in the municipality of Bussang. It flows to Koblenz and there empties into the **Rhine river** system. 

**Common depression:** Ultimately, both the Meuse and the Rhine flow into the **North Sea** , where the water from the High Fens reunites.

## 3. The High Fens river system
The High Fens is an important European watershed between the Meuse and the Rhine.
