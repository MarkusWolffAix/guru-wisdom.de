# Dragon Island
## The Guardian of Arillas

The small island of Gravia, affectionately called "Dragon Island" by travelers and locals alike, lies off the coast of Arillas in Corfu like an ancient secret. Those who gaze across the crystal-clear waters of the Ionian Sea in the soft light of the setting sun inevitably recognize the rocky silhouette of a colossal, sleeping dragon. But this mystical being is not a monster from bygone days, but a wise, elemental guardian who, eons ago, chose to protect this peaceful bay for all eternity.

In its deep meditation, the dragon does not sleep passively, but maintains an unwavering, cosmic focus. This concentrated, peaceful alignment stabilizes the entire energetic field of the bay. Especially in moments when life on the shore is loud and vibrant—filled with dance, community, and celebrations—this gigantic rock in the water forms a powerful anchor of perfect, untouched stillness. It reminds those who linger on its shore that true power need not be loud or destructive, but can lie in complete surrender to silence.

In this dragon-like stillness, a profound, protective gentleness is revealed. The grounding presence of the massive rock reflects the spiritual quality of the ancient seed syllable *Ma*. The sleeping dragon teaches us how to draw strength from deep inner grounding to develop a nurturing, supportive presence. It is about being both soft and strong, becoming a protective force for ourselves and for the world. Whoever opens their heart to this ancient, stone being in the sea inevitably feels the invitation to expand their own inner space so that even the wildest primal forces within us can find peace.
