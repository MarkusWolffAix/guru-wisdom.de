# NUN
## The Absolute Origin in Egypt and Mesopotamia

The question of the absolute origin of all being inevitably leads to the image of primordial water in humanity's oldest myths. When we examine the cosmology of ancient Egypt and early Mesopotamia, we encounter a fascinating convergence: In both worlds, the sound and the principle of NUN represent the absolute origin. The Egyptian *Nun* describes the primordial cosmic ocean, while the Sumerian *Nun.ki* (the sacred city of Eridu) marks the earthly zero point of civilization above the freshwater ocean.

### The Historical Truth: No Linguistic Relationship

Before delving into the depths of this parallel, a historical fact must be clearly stated: There is absolutely no linguistic, etymological, or cultural connection between the Egyptian word *Nun* and the Sumerian *Nun.ki*. That these two terms sound the same to us today is a pure coincidence of linguistic history.

The Egyptian *nw* or *nnw* originates from the Afroasiatic language family and specifically means the undulating, formless primordial waters before creation. Sumerian, on the other hand, is an isolated language. The name *NUN.KI* is composed of two completely independent cuneiform characters: *NUN* means "prince," "noble," or "exalted"; *KI* means "place" or "earth." *Nun.ki* is thus simply "The Noble Place."

The Sumerians and Egyptians formed these terms independently of each other. But it is precisely this independence that makes the deeper structural insight all the more profound: Both cultures, completely isolated from one another, came to the conclusion that all being originates from the unfathomable water and placed the sound NUN at the absolute beginning of their world.

### NUN as the Absolute Zero Point

In both traditions, the essence of Genesis resonates in NUN. There is no "before." Without Nun, there is no creation, no order, and no being.

At the heart of Egyptian teachings lies Nun as the absolute cosmic zero point. It is not "nothingness," but rather the infinite, formless primordial ocean of pure potentiality. Before space, time, gods, or humans existed, there was only this boundless body of water. The first order (Ra or Atum) does not create Nun, but awakens from it.

Looking to Mesopotamia, we encounter the same claim to the absolute beginning on an earthly plane. In the Sumerian king lists, Eridu (Nunki) is the very first city. The absolute zero point of earthly order manifests itself here. Nunki was founded directly above the Apsu, the cosmic freshwater ocean. This "noble place" is the first point of land where the infinite depths of water were grasped by the god Enki and structured into a tangible world.

### The Structural Parallel: From Potentiality to Form

Although linguistically unrelated, the Egyptian and Sumerian principles unite in perfect archetypal symmetry. They describe the transition from boundless latency to concrete manifestation. The Egyptian NUN is the absolute macrocosm, the cosmic origin of the universe—infinite, formless, and undulating. It *is* water itself. The Sumerian NUN.KI is the first microcosm, the earthly origin of world order—limited, formed, and architectural. It *rests upon* water.

The Sumerian suffix ".ki" (place/earth) here acts almost as a philosophical complement to the Egyptian principle: The infinite potential (NUN) condenses into a tangible place in the here and now (KI).

### Spiritual Reflection

For our own inner development, these ancient myths are timeless mirrors. We carry both principles within us. Your inner Nun is the first conscious action, the first thought, the first structure you build in your life. It is your "noble place" in the world. True creative power arises where you are aware of your infinite origin and yet summon the courage to boldly and clearly shape your life in concrete terms. And NOW 😀?
