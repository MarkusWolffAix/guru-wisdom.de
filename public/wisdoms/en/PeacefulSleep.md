# Evening affirmations
## For a peaceful sleep

These phrases help to consciously end the day, calm the nervous system, and focus the mind on rest and abundance.

### 1. The focus on abundance

"I am grateful for everything I have received today, for what I have realized, and for what I have not yet perceived."

Expressing gratitude before sleep programs the mind to see abundance. And someone who wakes up with an awareness of abundance attracts different things than someone who wakes up with an awareness of lack.

### 2. Conscious letting go

"I let go of everything I haven't solved today."

What you don't consciously let go of, your body unconsciously carries during sleep, and you wake up feeling heavier than when you fell asleep.

### 3. The positive orientation

"Tomorrow I will wake up renewed, with clarity and ready for what is to come."

Your subconscious mind does not distinguish between what is real and what is spoken with intensity.

### 4. Emotional cleansing

"I forgive myself and others for everything that didn't go perfectly today."

Those who consciously let go of resentment, frustration, and self-criticism before falling asleep lower the release of stress hormones and allow the mind to dive into deeper sleep phases without emotional baggage.

### 5. Strengthen basic trust

"I trust the flow of life and know that I am safe and secure in this moment."

A consciously induced feeling of safety signals to the autonomic nervous system that it may switch from alert mode to the parasympathetic resting state.

### 6. Physical Appreciation

"My body has done its best today, and I now allow it to fully relax and heal."

Consciously appreciating one's own body directs attention away from constant brooding and towards physical presence, releasing unconscious muscle tension.

### 7. The mental closure

"I end this day in peace; everything that was important today has been done."

This prevents the Zeigarnik effect – the brain's tendency to cling to unfinished tasks. It sends a clear signal to the mind that its shift is over for the day.

### 8. Unconditional self-worth

"I am valuable and whole, regardless of how much I have accomplished or achieved today."

It decouples self-worth from daily productivity and reduces the pressure of having to think about to-do lists in bed.

### 9. Delegating responsibility

"I now hand back the responsibility for the outside world to her and allow myself to simply be."

This conscious boundary-setting protects one's own mental energy and creates an inviolable space where one doesn't have to worry about anything except oneself.

### 10. Radical Acceptance

"Everything that happened today is allowed to be as it was. I no longer resist the past."

End the mental struggle and the attempt to rewrite the day in your mind. This saves cognitive energy and speeds up falling asleep.

### 11. Physiological relaxation

"With each exhale, I sink deeper into tranquility and transfer my weight to the surface beneath me."

Focusing on physical relaxation and prolonged exhalation stimulates the vagus nerve, slows the heartbeat and promotes falling asleep on a physical level.

**Practical tip:** Every evening, intuitively choose 1 to 3 affirmations that best match what you experienced that day or what you want to let go of.
