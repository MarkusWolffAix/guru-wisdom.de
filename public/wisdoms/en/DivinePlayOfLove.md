# The Divine Play
## The Return to the Primal Joy of Love

The journey to the Higher Self is not an escape from the world, but rather a conscious and joyful turning towards it. In yoga, union with the Divine does not describe a rigid concept of fulfilling obligations, but rather the realization of life as a responsible, loving game. It is an invitation to rediscover the origin of our being—the pure, unbridled primal joy of love—and to experience it with every breath.

Often we seek fulfillment externally, but the true turning point lies in understanding and embodying deep gratitude. When we begin to feel genuine appreciation for creation, our minds open. This interest is not limited to the spiritual; it encompasses admiration for science, fascination with history, and unconditional compassion for all sentient beings. Those who move through the world with this awake, interested gaze no longer merely think about joy—they become it. From this deeply felt mindfulness, love springs naturally. It grows first as gentle compassion for oneself and then expands from there, like the far-reaching root system of a strong tree, into the entire surrounding environment. One not only feels love, but also has the courage to show it openly and radiantly, without ever losing the responsibility one bears towards it. We recognize ourselves as loving, grounded co-creators.

A particularly powerful expression of this connection is revealed in the joy of giving and helping. Helping here is not perceived as a moral obligation, but as a natural overflow of one's own inner abundance. Those who give from a place of primal joy nourish the cycle of life. Thus, every act of care becomes a direct experience of the divine.

But the divine play unfolds not only in service to others, but also in the deep immersion in one's own joyful creativity. When we cook, mindfully transforming the wild herbs and bounty of the earth; when we paint, shaping colors into new worlds; or when we make music, uniting the warm sound of acoustic strings with our voices, we touch the very core of creation. In these moments of creative devotion, we are fully present in the here and now. Art, cooking, and music become meditative acts in which primal joy flows directly through us, becoming visible, tangible, or audible. In the pure joy of our own creativity, we celebrate the miracle of life.

## Lyrics
### The Divine Game

[Verse 1]
No departure from the world, no distant salvation,
we carry our tomorrow home through everyday life.

In the breath lies a beginning, still and clear,
love has always been there.

[Pre-Chorus]
When I bow before what surrounds me,
before every being that feels and lives,
then questions become an awakened gaze,
and joy finds its way back to me.

[Chorus]
The divine game begins in my hand,
in gratitude for earth, time, and humankind.

I give what overflows within me,
until love takes root and moves on.

The divine game, so near, so free,
primordial joy breathes through us two.

[Verse 2]
I learn to be gentle with myself,
no stone in my heart must remain forever.

From small gestures grows a strong tree,
its shade becomes open space.


[Pre-Chorus]
And when a stranger's heart calls for closeness,
I don't diminish when my abundance flows.

A plate, a moment, an honest glance,
give life back its radiance.

[Chorus]
The divine game begins in my hand,
in gratitude for earth, time, and humankind.

I give what overflows within me,
until love takes root and spreads.

The divine game, so near, so free,
primordial joy breathes through us two.

[Bridge]
In cutting herbs, in the red on the leaf,
in the sound that has found its place in the quiet room,
I become a source, I become space,
and every moment carries its dream.

[Final Chorus]
The divine game resounds in every being,
in gratitude, everything may be greater.

We are the hands that gently sow,
that visibly carry love through life.

The divine play, so near, so free,
primordial joy breathes through us two.

[Outro]
In the here and now, in this bright moment,
joy returns to us.
