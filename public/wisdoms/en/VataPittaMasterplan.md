## Keeping the doshas in balance

This comprehensive dossier brings together all the insights for harmonizing your mixed dosha. Because you combine the speed of Vata (air/ether) with the intensity of Pitta (fire/water), your focus is on grounding without heaviness and cooling without coldness.

# The Doshas
## The general principle of operation

Doshas are bio-energies that govern all physical and mental processes. Every person possesses an individual basic constitution (Prakriti).

* **Vata (Movement):** Air & Ether. Responsible for the nervous system, respiration, and movement. Qualities: Cold, dry, light, mobile.
* **Pitta (Transformation):** Fire & Water. Responsible for metabolism, digestion (Agni), and intellect. Qualities: Hot, pungent, oily.
* **Kapha (structure):** Earth & Water. Responsible for stability, joint lubrication, and the immune system. Properties: Heavy, stable, cool, moist.

# Specific analysis
## Your Vata-Pitta profile

As a mixed type, you are a dynamic powerhouse. You possess the alert mind of wind and the sharp intellect of fire. However, you must be careful not to "burn out" (Pitta) or "fly away" (Vata).

* **The 80% rule:** Never give 100%. A 20% reserve protects you from burnout and irritability.
* **Carbonated water:** Absolutely avoid it. It acts like trapped wind, causes Vayu disturbances (flatulence) and burning acid irritation in the mouth and stomach.
* **Sauerkraut:** Reduces Vata due to its heavy grounding effect, but increases Pitta due to the strong acidity/heat of fermentation. If consumed at all, only in very small quantities.

# Oil science
## The external and internal application

In Ayurveda, oils (Sneha) mean "love" and are essential for your nervous system.

* **Almond oil:** Your ideal all-rounder. Harmonizes Vata and Pitta equally, intensively nourishes nerves and skin. Perfect for foot massage.
* **Sesame oil:** Strongly warming. The best remedy for Vata cold and anxiety (autumn/winter). Ideal for morning oil pulling. Can be stored at room temperature.
* **Coconut oil:** Intensely cooling. Your "Pitta firefighter" in the height of summer. Perfect for oil pulling and massage in the heat. (Smoke point of cold-pressed oil approx. 200°C).
* **Olive oil:** Calms Vata (heavy/warming), rich in monounsaturated fatty acids (Omega-9). (Smoke point cold-pressed 130°-190°C, refined 230°C).

# Mudra practice
## Energetic hand gestures

# Vayu Mudra
## Calming of the winds
**Execution:** Thumb rests on the middle joint of the index finger.  
**Effect:** Warms the cold and the air in cases of dominant Vata. Stops racing thoughts and provides extremely fast relief from bloating.

# Prithvi Mudra
## Strengthening the Earth
**Execution:** Ring fingertip touches thumb tip.  
**Effect:** Provides stability, grounds nervous Vata and takes away aggression from Pitta ambition.

# Apana Mudra
## Power of cleaning
**Execution:** Middle finger and ring finger touch the tip of the thumb.  
**Effect:** Supports detoxification and the important downward flow of energy (digestion).

# Morning routine
## The Path to Clarity (Dinacharya)

1. **Tongue cleaning:** Gently remove coating (Ama) to support detoxification.
2. **Oil pulling (Gandusha):** Pull warm oil (sesame in winter, coconut in summer) through your teeth for 5 minutes.
3. **The inner bath:** A glass of warm, still water (possibly with lemon). Never carbonated water!
4. **Meditation:** Hold Vayu Mudra for 5-10 minutes, breathing deeply and calmly into the abdomen.
5. **Mini-Abhyanga:** Short rubbing of the soles of the feet and face with harmonizing almond oil.

---

# Evening routine
## Gentle grounding (Ratri Charya)

The evening is critical in order to protect Pitta from "second wind" and to ground Vata.

1. **Grounding dinner:** Warm and easily digestible (e.g., mung dal, sweet potato), no later than 7:00 p.m. No heavy raw food!
2. **Digital detox:** Turn off screens from 8:30 pm onwards. Blue light triggers Pitta activity.
3. **Padabhyanga (foot massage):** Massage the feet with almond oil to draw the heat and energy from the head down into the earth.
4. **Moon Milk:** Warm (plant-based) milk with cardamom, cinnamon and a little ghee.
5. **Triphala:** ½ tsp of Triphala with warm water before bedtime for gentle bowel cleansing.
6. **Sleep preparation:** Hold the Prithvi Mudra in bed to invite heaviness for sleep.
